CONTROLS

GAMEPLAY

Survive enemy waves and eliminate all targets. Manage your ammo and health carefully.

Enemies patrol the map and will engage when they spot you. Take cover and use the environment to your advantage.

Complete objectives to progress through waves. Each wave gets progressively harder.

WEAPONS & ITEMS

Collect ammo pickups and armor to stay in the fight. Your armor absorbs damage first.
